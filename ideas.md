# Harborline Communication Ops — Design Direction

## Three possible directions

| Theme Name | Very Brief Intro | Probability |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| **Regulated Operations Command Center** | A dark-navy control shell and paper-white workspace make human approval, evidence, and controlled distribution feel calm and serious. | 0.07 |
| **Field Sales Fieldbook** | An editorial, route-led interface emphasizes account relationships and the practical work of a representative in the field. | 0.04 |
| **Marketplace Signal Desk** | A lighter trade-execution system foregrounds product discovery, buyer requests, and portfolio movement. | 0.09 |

## Chosen direction: Regulated Operations Command Center

**Design Movement:** Modern industrial editorial systems and air-traffic / maritime control-room information design.

**Core Principles:** The primary decision is visually unmistakable; human judgment is more prominent than automation; every action leaves a visible record; and control states communicate meaning rather than decoration.

**Color Philosophy:** Ink navy represents system authority; Signal Vermilion is reserved for human action, intervention, and exceptions; sea-green is reserved for verified, qualified, or safe system states; paper-white preserves legibility for detailed evidence.

**Layout Paradigm:** A persistent command rail anchors navigation while a wide route-grid workspace moves from live decision to supporting context, evidence, and action. The application should not collapse into identical cards.

**Signature Elements:** Route-and-record geometry, connected status nodes, manifest-style metadata, and restrained audit stamps.

**Interaction Philosophy:** Actions are deliberate and specific; the interface always identifies the accountable owner and makes review gates visible. Reset and direct-entry states support reliable live demonstrations.

**Animation:** Use only short transform/opacity transitions for selection and action confirmation; no decorative motion; respect reduced-motion preferences.

**Typography System:** Manrope supplies decisive operational headings; IBM Plex Mono supplies audit labels, record IDs, and technical metadata. Headlines are assertive; evidence is compact and scannable.

**Brand Essence:** Harborline turns relationship-sensitive distributor communication into a controlled, human-accountable operating record. **Precise, accountable, composed.**

**Brand Voice:** Headlines identify a judgment boundary, not a generic benefit. CTAs describe the controlled action. Examples: “Run the right message. Keep the relationship human.” and “Verify terms & audience.”

**Wordmark & Logo:** The Harborline mark is a minimal route-and-node signal: a coral human-decision node linked to sea-green verified nodes, used beside a compact letter-spaced wordmark.

**Signature Brand Color:** **Signal Vermilion** (`#e6533c`).

## Style Decisions

- Harborline is a **regulated operations command center**, not a generic SaaS dashboard.
- Copy is precise, accountable, and human-in-the-loop; use “sandbox” and “no live delivery” rather than repetitive generic demo language.
- Route-and-record geometry must recur across workflow, reply, account, and audit states.
- Coral means human intervention/action; sea-green means verified/system-safe; navy means system authority.
