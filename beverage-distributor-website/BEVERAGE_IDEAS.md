# Beverage Distributor Website — Design Direction

## Three Possible Directions

### Approach 1
**Theme Name:** Route Thread

**Very Brief Intro:** A field-to-account editorial system that follows a real distributor moment from route observation to accountable handoff. It makes operational rigor feel personal because every visual starts with a recognizable account interaction.

**Probability:** 0.073

### Approach 2
**Theme Name:** Back-Bar Ledger

**Very Brief Intro:** A warmer hospitality-led composition that moves between the back bar, the shelf, and the account notebook. It emphasizes buyer trust and local market knowledge.

**Probability:** 0.041

### Approach 3
**Theme Name:** Delivery Window

**Very Brief Intro:** A practical route-and-fulfillment visual language built around the narrow windows in which beverage account work gets done. It is direct, compact, and operationally energetic.

**Probability:** 0.086

## Chosen Approach: Route Thread

### Design Movement

**Editorial field notes meet beverage route operations.** The page becomes a sequence of account moments, not a generic software brochure. It uses tactile route materials, account artifacts, and a subtle connective line to show how field context moves into a responsible next action.

### Core Principles

1. **Begin where the rep begins.** Every story starts with a cooler, shelf, menu, route, display, or buyer conversation—never an abstract workflow tile.
2. **Keep account context intact.** The design visually carries a moment, its account, its named owner, and its boundary together.
3. **Respect local relationship work.** The experience should feel useful to a team that wins one account, one buyer, and one market at a time.
4. **Show disciplined assistance.** The product prepares and routes work; pricing, quantity, allocation, terms, and sensitive account judgment stay unmistakably human-owned.

### Color Philosophy

Route Paper, warm glass amber, road gray, and deep navy create an environment that feels close to a real field day. **Signal Blue** remains the ownable connective color. Rust identifies a decision or exception, like a marked-up field note rather than a system alarm.

### Layout Paradigm

The website follows a **route thread**: field signal → account context → named handoff → retained operating memory. A slim reference rail preserves chronology, while sections alternate between broad editorial copy and imagery from off-premise retail, on-premise hospitality, and physical account artifacts.

### Signature Elements

1. A continuous cobalt route line that links field material to the account record.
2. Small timestamp and account-type notes that make each section feel observed rather than generalized.
3. Framed real-world scene fragments—cooler, display plan, menu edge, route note—without using real brands or customers.

### Interaction Philosophy

Workflow tabs behave like account-moment records. Visitors can switch among a route rhythm, display handoff, and on-premise follow-up, seeing what was noticed, who owns the response, and which decision remains protected.

### Animation

Use short 180–260ms reveal transitions and a gentle route-line draw only when content becomes visible. Hovering a workflow record gives a slight lateral pull and state clarity. Motion remains entirely optional under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Typography System

**Instrument Serif** handles the relationship-led promise and reflective moments. **Manrope** keeps working copy direct and operational. **IBM Plex Mono** is reserved for account type, time, owner, and control labels. The type should feel like a refined account notebook, not a campaign deck.

### Brand Essence

**Communication Ops gives beverage distributors a better way to carry real account moments from the field to the right person and next action.**

**Personality:** grounded, attentive, exacting.

### Brand Voice

Copy speaks in recognizable account realities, without overclaiming. It avoids generic automation language and names the relationship that needs protecting.

> “The account moment starts in the field. Keep it moving all the way to the buyer.”

> “Your route knows the account. Your operation should remember the moment.”

### Wordmark & Logo

The established Communication Ops routing-junction mark is retained to preserve product continuity. In this version, it appears alongside a more human field-note lockup and route-derived document details rather than being renamed.

### Signature Brand Color

**Signal Blue — `#1F70E8`**. It is the route thread, the active field-to-account connection, and the clearest visual signal of prepared work.
