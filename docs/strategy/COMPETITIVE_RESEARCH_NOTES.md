## Early comparative observation

The reviewed market is **not empty**. Field CRM, ERP, distributor intelligence, sales recommendations, routes, orders, and BI are all actively addressed by known companies. The most defensible early Harborline hypothesis is therefore not “no one handles beverage sales.” It is narrower:

> A distributor may need a clear, human-governed operating layer that carries a routine account event through audience/eligibility, approved communication, delivery posture, reply exception, named ownership, and audit evidence—without requiring the distributor to replace its CRM or ERP.

This is an interpretation to validate with user interviews, not a verified claim that no competitor offers any element of the workflow.

## Browser confirmation

Visual review of the live AccountTouch, Ohanafy CRM, and Provi pages confirmed the category boundaries in the tables above. AccountTouch leads with a rep-facing mobile/field-sales experience; Ohanafy leads with a broad beverage CRM workspace; and Provi leads with a buyer-facing search, ordering, and rep-conversation experience. This supports the documented conclusion that Harborline should not imitate any one of those start pages, but should make the **governed account-communication workflow** its primary product surface. This is a design/positioning interpretation, not a claim of feature absence.
