# Competitive Research Framework — Harborline Communication Ops

## Research question

Which existing products already serve beverage-alcohol distributors, sales representatives, retailers, and communication workflows—and where could Harborline create a premium, coherent, trusted experience without pretending that every missing feature is a market opportunity?

## Solution categories

| Category | Typical job | Examples to verify | Relevance to Harborline |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Beverage sales execution / field CRM** | Help reps plan visits, capture activity, follow up, and manage accounts. | AccountTouch, Leadbeam. | Closest rep-facing alternatives. |
| **Beverage operating system / ERP** | Run orders, inventory, finance, route accounting, CRM, and broader distribution operations. | Ohanafy, Encompass. | Potential system-of-record or integration boundary. |
| **Wholesale distributor intelligence** | Recommend sales actions, analyze accounts, and identify growth opportunity. | Proton.ai, Overproof. | Adjacent insight layer and buyer expectation benchmark. |
| **Marketing / communication automation** | Segment audiences and deliver messages through email, SMS, or related channels. | Klaviyo, GoHighLevel, Twilio. | Delivery infrastructure, not the category-specific product experience. |

## Evaluation criteria

| Criterion | What will be verified | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Primary user** | Rep, manager, operations, retailer, supplier, or finance team. | Reveals whose daily work the product is actually improving. |
| **Core workflow** | Visit planning, follow-up, order management, messaging, recommendation, or compliance. | Prevents feature-by-feature comparison without context. |
| **Communication posture** | Drafting, task creation, direct delivery, segmentation, reply handling, or automation. | Determines whether Harborline has a real communication-workflow wedge. |
| **Human control** | Ownership, approval, escalation, auditability, or autonomy. | Central to the Harborline product promise. |
| **Data / integration posture** | System of record, Salesforce-native, external integration, or standalone overlay. | Defines the realistic implementation path. |
| **Buyer value** | Coverage, revenue workflow, rep productivity, data visibility, or risk reduction. | Clarifies why a distributor would care. |
| **Product experience** | Unified, category-specific, configurable, fragmented, or service-heavy. | Tests the “Apple of this industry” opportunity responsibly. |

## Research discipline

The study will prioritize company product pages, official documentation, customer material, and reputable reporting. **Verified capability** will be separated from **Harborline interpretation**. A missing public claim is not proof that a product lacks a feature; it only means the feature was not verified in reviewed public material.

## Harborline hypothesis to test

> Harborline may differentiate not by becoming another beverage CRM or a generic messaging tool, but by making category-specific, regulated, human-governed account communication feel unusually clear, coherent, and dependable—from trigger to named human handoff to evidence record.
