# Weekly Buyer-Validation Routine

## Purpose

This is the simplest operating rhythm for the next two weeks. Its job is to convert sales conversations into product evidence, not to create more meetings or more documentation.

## Monday: prepare the week

Spend 20 minutes selecting the people to contact and the single question each conversation should answer. Prioritize conversations that can validate a real workflow rather than vague enthusiasm.

| Owner | Action | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sales partner** | Ask warm contacts for 25-minute discovery conversations with a leader, rep, operations stakeholder, or retailer buyer. | A short list of confirmed interviews and roles. |
| **Project lead** | Open the demo at Workflow Center, confirm the Reorder Reminder reset state, and prepare the evidence-log template. | One consistent demonstration and a ready note form. |
| **Both** | Agree on which hypothesis matters most this week. | One interview focus, such as rep approval, retailer channel preference, or the source of no-reorder signals. |

## During each conversation

Use the 25-minute sequence in [`../product/BUYER_VALIDATION_PACKAGE.md`](../product/BUYER_VALIDATION_PACKAGE.md). Lead with a recent real example, then show the narrow workflow. Do not pitch every capability, promise integrations, quote prices, or argue with objections.

## Within 24 hours

Complete one section in [`INTERVIEW_EVIDENCE_LOG.md`](INTERVIEW_EVIDENCE_LOG.md). Preserve the participant’s exact wording, then separately record your interpretation and the product implication.

The sales partner owns the relationship follow-up. The project lead owns whether a finding changes the product, the pitch, the workflow boundary, or the pilot score.

## Friday: 30-minute evidence review

| Question | Decision to record |
| --- | --- |
| What repeated pain did people describe in their own words? | Keep, refine, or discard the first workflow hypothesis. |
| What approval posture did reps and buyers accept? | Draft only, rep-approved, policy-approved, or no proactive outreach. |
| What exception types must always route to a person? | Update the product’s non-negotiable exception policy. |
| Which existing system holds the needed context? | Define the likely client integration boundary. |
| Is there a real sponsor for a narrow pilot? | Update the Pilot Readiness Protocol score and next action. |

## End-of-round decision

After approximately eight conversations, use [`../product/PILOT_READINESS_PROTOCOL.md`](../product/PILOT_READINESS_PROTOCOL.md) to choose one of three directions: prepare a narrow client pilot, run targeted follow-up discovery, or revise the workflow hypothesis before building more.

> **The rule:** Do not expand the product because the demo receives compliments. Expand it only when repeated workflow evidence names the next control, screen, or integration requirement.
